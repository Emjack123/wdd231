NP Membership Details

Cost: Free (For registered Non-Profit Organizations)

  • Basic directory listing on the Chamber website.
  • Access to monthly community networking mixers.
  • Standard newsletter subscription.

Bronze Membership Details

Cost: $150 / Year

  • Enhanced business directory listing with logo.
  • Member-to-member discount program inclusion.
  • 2 tickets to annual business forum seminars.

Silver Membership Details

Cost: $300 / Year

  • All Bronze benefits included.
  • 10% discount on local event advertising spaces.
  • Free access to regional training workshops for up to 3 staff members.
  • Quarterly dynamic spotlight feature options.

Gold Membership Details

Cost: $500 / Year

  • All Silver benefits included.
  • Permanent homepage spotlight rotation.
  • Free access to all premium chamber events and VIP networking tables.
  • Complimentary booth at the annual Chamber Business Expo.
