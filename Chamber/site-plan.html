urban Ride: A Responsive e-trucking platform

Selected Name: Urban Ride , a responsive e-trucking platform

Reason for Selection: This name directly targets electric truck enthusiasts within Uyo Area. making it highly clear for branding, search optimization, and community recognition.

Optional Domain Availability: uyo-truck.org

Site Purpose

The site provides a central hub for Urban e-trucking operation. Its core scope is to catalog local trail specifications, deliver technical and hiring recommendations to potential investors.

Target Audience Scenarios

The content and layout of the website are designed to satisfy direct inquiries from potential investors, including:

  • Scenario 1: "What is the best type of e-truck and battery capacity to get for navigating swampy terrains?"
  • Scenario 2: "Where can I find direct contact information or social links of the director and hiring criteria ?"

Typography

To preserve clean visual hierarchies, the following font constraints have been set:

  • Heading Font: 'Roboto', sans-serif — Applied across all main title tokens, navigation anchors, and subsection headers for bold, modern readability.
  • Body Font: 'Open Sans', sans-serif — Applied to all general paragraph items, lists, and form labels to provide neutral, clean readability on small screens.
